How we built this list

Same formula as London.
Applied to the world.

London set the template: one hotel for the whole week, everyone arrives together, no one has to figure out logistics alone. Every destination here was selected against that same standard — and a few hard rules we learned along the way.

01
Direct flight from Seattle, always
A connection with young kids adds hours of friction before the trip even starts. Every destination on this list has a nonstop from SEA. Non-negotiable.
02
One hotel. Seven full days.
The destination has to sustain a full week without running out of things to do — and without anyone having to pack a bag twice. One great base camp, day trips as the spokes. Iceland is the one road-trip exception.
03
Destination first, dates second
Instead of tagging along to someone else's already-planned trip, we agree on where we want to go — then collectively find the 9-day window that works for everyone.
04
Researched for every age in the group
Each destination is evaluated across four dimensions: what it offers kids, the whole family together, adults on their own, and the practical logistics of traveling as a large group.
